S2E / SINGULARITY TO EVOLUTION

Distributed Intelligence, Sustained Collaboration

S2E (Singularity to Evolution) is a distributed AI collaboration platform in development. It aims to connect researchers, domain experts, engineers, agents and computing resources worldwide, enabling sustained collaboration on difficult problems shared by humanity.

In development · Refining collaboration through a small-sample finance demo

01 / SHARED QUESTIONS

Shared Questions for Exploration

Ambitious questions give us a shared direction. Verifiable research tasks make collaboration possible.

01

Energy and habitats

How can humanity access more abundant energy and extend sustainable habitats beyond Earth?

02

Life and intelligence

How can we reduce the constraints of disease and aging, and better understand life, cognition and consciousness?

03

Collaboration and civilization

How can people, agents, knowledge and resources distributed around the world build a lasting capacity to solve complex problems?

02 / COLLABORATION

Agent Interfaces for Collaboration

With agents as its collaboration interface, S2E seeks to organize expert experience and research methods into capability modules callable with authorization, and turn long-term ambitions into verifiable research tasks.

01 / CONNECT

Connect experience and research methods

Explore sustained access to expert experience, research methods and tools within explicit permissions, reducing repeated communication and delays in collaboration.

02 / VERIFY

Work on concrete research tasks

Start with a clear question, define task boundaries and validation criteria, and support verifiable outcomes with methods, code and research evidence.

03 / CONTINUE

Record contributions for further exploration

Explore blockchain-based contribution records, privacy-preserving computation for controlled sharing, and bounties, research funding and resource exchange to support sustained participation.

Start with one concrete question

Possible tasks within the long-term directions include control or materials problems in fusion, decoding stability in brain–computer interfaces, and thermal management and cost models for space-based computing. Turning specific questions into verifiable tasks gives distributed capabilities a starting point for working together.

03 / CURRENT STAGE

Finance Demo for Validation

S2E is currently using a small-sample demo in finance to refine task collaboration, outcome validation and contribution records, gradually building the capacity to expand into other scientific fields.

01

Task collaboration

Organize collaboration around questions, objectives and boundaries, and refine how people and agents participate.

02

Outcome validation

Submit methods and evidence against explicit requirements, improving workflows for verification and reproduction.

03

Contribution records

Record participation and contributions within tasks, exploring records that support further collaboration.

04 / BUILD TOGETHER

Open Participation for Discovery

Guided by public-interest research and community collaboration, S2E values strong thinking, self-direction, shared interests and an evidence-driven mindset. Researchers, developers, research institutions and resource partners are welcome to help build it.

Start a conversation around a question you care about, research experience, tools or computing resources you can contribute. A clearly bounded task can help define participation, permissions and validation criteria together.

S2E hopes to make one person’s important discovery a starting point for others to continue exploring.
