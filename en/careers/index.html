Careers & Collaboration

Research and Engineering Together

Participation options include full-time roles, internships and collaboration tasks in research and engineering. Office location is unrestricted and remote collaboration is supported. The focus is on technical contributors capable of translating research questions into verifiable outcomes.

Full-timeInternshipsCollaboration tasksRemote

Talent profile

Talent criteria focus on research and engineering capabilities for complex problem solving and system development.

Complex problem solving

Capabilities include complex problem analysis, continued improvement of algorithms, systems and experimental workflows, and sustained progress in challenging research and engineering settings.

Problem-driven research

Continued examination of problem causes, boundary conditions and opportunities for system improvement supports verifiable task outcomes.

Research and engineering capabilities

Rigorous engineering practice is combined with hypothesis development, experiment design, literature analysis and method iteration to translate research hypotheses into verifiable systems.

Continuous learning and iteration

Continuous learning and evidence-based refinement of methods support sustained improvement of system capabilities.

Ways to participate

Multiple ways to contribute to shared research

Full-time

For researchers and engineers able to assume sustained responsibility for core work and long-term research priorities.

Internship

For focused work on a clear question with a verifiable outcome.

Collaboration task

For independent researchers, engineers or teams working within a defined task boundary.

Collaboration arrangements: Remote collaboration is supported, with no restriction on office city. Public collaboration locations include Hangzhou, Qingdao, Shenzhen, Hong Kong and Singapore.

Suggested contact materials

  • Priority research or engineering topics;
  • one or two representative projects, papers, code repositories or work samples demonstrating relevant capabilities and judgment;
  • the proposed participation format, availability and collaboration arrangements.
