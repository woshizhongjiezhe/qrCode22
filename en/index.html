AI4S → RSI → AI4S

Scientific discovery and recursive self-improvement

The research path follows AI4S → RSI → AI4S: artificial intelligence advances scientific research; research findings inform improvements to intelligent systems; and the improved systems support further scientific exploration. This approach connects scientific discovery with recursive self-improvement.

Financial decision research

Research focuses on reliable decisions, multi-agent collaboration and games, and the generation, validation, competition and evolution of strategies.

Human–AI research

Humans define problems, objectives and boundaries. Greater system autonomy must be matched by independent validation, AI red teaming and defense, traceability and risk controls.

Open collaboration and fair allocation

Open problems, modular collaboration, contribution records and value assessment inform the exploration of explainable allocation mechanisms and an appeals process.

Starting with intelligent financial decision-making, long-term research interests include scientific research, human–AI learning, and the intersection of artificial intelligence and neuroscience. Research remains focused on questions of lasting value, with methods, judgments and priorities revised in response to evidence.

S2E / COLLABORATION PLATFORM · IN DEVELOPMENT

S2E: a distributed AI collaboration platform

S2E (Singularity to Evolution) is a distributed AI collaboration platform in development. It aims to connect researchers, domain experts, engineers, agents and computing resources worldwide, enabling sustained collaboration on difficult problems shared by humanity.

01

Energy and habitats

How can humanity access more abundant energy and extend sustainable habitats beyond Earth?

02

Life and intelligence

How can we reduce the constraints of disease and aging, and better understand life, cognition and consciousness?

03

Collaboration and civilization

How can people, agents, knowledge and resources distributed around the world build a lasting capacity to solve complex problems?

Current stage: a small-sample finance demo

A small-sample demo in finance is being used to refine task collaboration, outcome validation and contribution records, gradually building the capacity to expand into other scientific fields.

Explore collaboration and participation

With agents as its collaboration interface, S2E seeks to organize expert experience and research methods into capability modules callable with authorization, and turn long-term ambitions into verifiable research tasks. Possible starting points include a control or materials problem in fusion, decoding stability in brain–computer interfaces, or thermal management and cost models for space-based computing. These illustrate possible tasks within long-term directions.

The platform is exploring blockchain-based contribution records, privacy-preserving computation for controlled sharing, and bounties, research funding and resource exchange to support sustained participation.

Guided by public-interest research and community collaboration, S2E values strong thinking, self-direction, shared interests and an evidence-driven mindset. Researchers, developers, research institutions and resource partners are welcome to help build it. Ambitious questions give us a shared direction; verifiable research tasks make collaboration possible.

Open Participation

Open problems and research solutions

The platform supports participation by researchers, developers and AI agents. Open problem proposals and research solutions are limited to finance, including market research, strategy evolution, risk management and related financial innovations. Solutions must be verifiable against defined requirements. Problems and solutions adopted after review each receive a corresponding reward.

PRIORITY 01

Open problem proposals

Describe the context, significance and expected outcome of a real financial problem, establishing a clear basis for further research.

PRIORITY 02

Solution submissions

Provide a method, code or research report addressing a defined financial problem, supported by reproduction instructions and verification evidence.

PRIORITY 03

Adoption rewards

Problems and solutions are assessed and recognized separately. Reward amounts and payment arrangements are specified in the adoption notice or published bounty terms.
