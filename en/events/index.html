The 8th International Conference on Distributed Artificial Intelligence (DAI 2026)

The 8th International Conference on Distributed Artificial Intelligence (DAI 2026) will take place from November 29 to December 2, 2026, at City University of Hong Kong in Hong Kong, China. Under the theme “Agentic AI Goes Live — Science, Systems, and Societies”, the conference will explore advances in agent technologies across scientific research, systems development and societal applications.

As a sponsoring partner, SingularityX looks forward to joining researchers, developers and industry peers from around the world in Hong Kong to discuss the future of distributed intelligence and AI agents.
